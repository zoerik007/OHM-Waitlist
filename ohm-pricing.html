Frequently asked

What exactly does OHM certify — the creator or the work? +
OHM certifies the work — not just the creator. Each certification covers one specific piece of work submitted with its process documentation. The creator's identity is verified and hashed, but what goes on the blockchain is the fingerprint of that specific work. This means each work has its own provenance record, independent of others.
How long does the review process take? +
Maker tier: typically 5–7 business days. Studio tier: priority review within 48 hours. Enterprise: dedicated auditor with SLA. During our founding period (first 500 creators) we're targeting faster turnaround while we build out the auditor network. You'll receive email updates at every step.
What if I use tools like Grammarly or Lightroom? Does that disqualify me? +
No. Productivity tools, spell checkers, grammar tools, photo editing software, DAWs, and IDEs are all permitted. The line is between tools that assist a human creator and AI systems that generate the primary content. Our full policy document covers this in detail with specific examples for every creative category. If you're unsure about something specific, email policy@onlyhumanmade.org before submitting.
Can I cancel at any time? +
Yes — no contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel any time from your account settings. Your existing certified works remain in the public registry permanently, even after cancellation. The blockchain record is immutable. What you lose on cancellation is the ability to submit new works for certification and access to the member portal and community.
What is the founding member discount? +
The first 500 creators to join OHM receive 50% off their chosen tier for the lifetime of their membership. This is not a promotional rate that expires — it's a permanent thank-you for being part of the foundation. Founding member pricing is applied automatically at checkout; no code needed. Once the 500 spots are filled, standard pricing applies to all new members.
What happens if my certification is rejected? +
If an auditor determines your submission doesn't meet the human-made standard, you'll receive detailed written feedback explaining why. You can revise and resubmit — one free resubmission is included per certification attempt. If you believe the decision was made in error, our appeals process allows you to request a second auditor review within 30 days.
