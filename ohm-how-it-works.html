The certification process

Six steps to a seal
that means something.

Every OHM certification goes through the same rigorous process — because the seal is only worth anything if the process behind it is unimpeachable.

6 Steps
48hr Studio turnaround
∞ Permanent record
1

Submission

You submit your work and process documentation

You complete the submission form with your work file, your creative category, a written description of your process, and your process documentation — the evidence of how the work was made. This is not a bureaucratic formality. The documentation is the foundation of your certification's credibility.

What counts as documentation

Procreate timelapses · Photoshop history · Ableton session files · Word revision history · Git commits · WIP photos · Raw files with EXIF · Handwritten notes · Darkroom contact sheets

What you also declare

Your AI disclosure — whether any AI tools were used, and if so, in what capacity. Honest disclosure is required. Our policy covers every grey zone in detail.

2

Forensics

Metadata and file forensics

Before a human auditor ever sees your submission, our system runs a technical analysis of your files — checking metadata, file creation timestamps, edit histories, EXIF data, and file signatures for consistency with your declared process. This isn't an AI detector. It's a consistency check.

What we check

File creation and modification timestamps · Camera EXIF data · Edit history consistency · File format signatures · Metadata field completeness

What we don't do

We do not use AI content detectors. They are unreliable and getting less reliable every month. Forensics flags inconsistencies — the human auditor makes the judgment.

3

Human review

A domain expert reviews your work

This is what makes OHM different from every other certification or registry. A human auditor — someone who actually works in your creative field — reviews your submission. A writer reviews writing. An illustrator reviews illustration. A musician reviews music. They read your process description, examine your documentation, and make a judgment: does this work meet the human-made standard?

Our auditors are

Working professionals in their creative field · Trained in the OHM certification policy · Paid fairly for their expert time · Bound by a non-disclosure agreement

The auditor looks for

Evidence of genuine human creative decision-making · Consistency between the work and the process · Authenticity of the documentation · Honest AI disclosure

4

Legal foundation

You sign the Maker's Oath

Before your certification is issued, you sign the Maker's Oath — a legally binding declaration of human authorship. This is not a terms and conditions checkbox. It creates genuine legal liability for false certification, which is what makes it credible. The Oath is the mechanism by which the OHM seal derives its legal weight and its trustworthiness.

The Oath covers

Declaration of primary human authorship · Confirmation of AI disclosure · Consent to spot audits · Acknowledgment of legal consequences for false certification

Consequences of false oath

Immediate decertification of all works · Permanent ban from OHM · Public notice of violation · Civil legal action

5

Permanent record

Your work is written to the blockchain

On certification approval, a cryptographic record of your work is written permanently to the Polygon blockchain. A SHA-256 hash of your certified file — a unique 64-character fingerprint — is timestamped and stored in an immutable public ledger. This record cannot be altered, deleted, or forged. It will outlast OHM, outlast any platform, and outlast any algorithm.

What goes on chain

SHA-256 hash of the certified file · Hashed creator identity · Timestamp · Certification tier · OHM registry URI

What stays off chain

Your name, personal details, and the file itself. Privacy-preserving by design. Only the fingerprint is public — not the file it belongs to.

6

The seal

Your OHM seal is issued. The work is yours to prove.

Your certification is live. You receive your OHM seal — a digital badge you can place on your portfolio, your social profiles, your invoices, your prints. Anyone who sees it can verify it instantly at onlyhumanmade.org/verify. And because it's on the blockchain, it will still be verifiable in twenty years.

What you receive

OHM seal (digital + print) · Registry entry URL · Blockchain transaction hash · Embeddable verification widget · Public creator profile listing

Ongoing obligations

You may be selected for a random spot audit at any time. Cooperating with spot audits is required to maintain certification. The process is the same as the original review.

The registry entry

What a certified work looks like on the blockchain

Every certified work generates a public registry entry that anyone can verify. The hash is the fingerprint — unique to that exact file. Change a single pixel and the hash changes completely. That's what makes it unforgeable.
