The analogy that explains everything

Then — 2003

When Digital Became Infinite,
Film Became
Valuable.

When infinite, free digital photos became possible, something unexpected happened: film photography surged. Lomography, large format, darkroom printing — not in spite of digital, but because of it.

The constraint became the value. The grain, the patience, the 36-frame roll — these weren't inefficiencies to be eliminated. They were proof of investment. They were what made the image matter.

Now — 2026

When AI Made
Everything,
Human Work
Became Rare.

We are at that same inflection point — but for all creative work. AI can generate infinite writing, music, illustration, and code. Instantly. For free.

Which means human-made work is now what film photography became: scarce, deliberate, traceable, warm. The question isn't whether human creativity has value. It's whether buyers can find it — and trust it.

That's what the OHM seal is for.

How certification works

Six steps to the seal.

01
Submit your process

Draft files, edit histories, session logs, sketches. Show us the work behind the work — timestamped and layered.

02
Metadata forensics

Our tools analyze file signatures, creation curves, and tool fingerprints. AI-generated files leave patterns humans don't.

03
Human auditor review

A domain expert — always a human — reviews your submission. A writer reads writing. A musician listens to music.

04
Maker's oath

You sign a legally binding declaration of human authorship. The threat of consequence is what makes the seal trustworthy.

05
Registry entry

A cryptographic hash of your certified work is written to a public ledger. Permanent, unforgeable provenance.

06
Ongoing audits

Spot re-verification keeps the seal current. Your certification is alive, not just historical.

About OnlyHumanMade

Founder  ·  Photo credit: @michaelxgabe

Our mission

To make human authorship
verifiable, valuable,
and permanent.

Ricardo is a Creative Director and film photographer based in Austin, Texas — a decade-plus of professional work spent sharpening an eye for what makes an image feel real. That eye is exactly what made one moment stop everything: a client looked at a photo shot on 35mm film and asked if it was AI-generated.

That question became the seed for OnlyHumanMade — an independent certification body built to give creators a verifiable way to prove their work is genuinely human-made.

We exist because the internet has always rewarded the loudest signal, not the most truthful one, and by 2025 the gap between human-made and machine-made had vanished entirely. The OHM seal isn't anti-technology — it's pro-human, asking creators not to reject their tools, but to claim authorship: to stand behind their work with their name, their process, and their oath. That act of claiming is what we certify.

Rigor over convenience

Certification takes effort by design. If it were easy, it would mean nothing. Every step of our process exists because trust has to be earned — not assumed.

Creators first, always

We are a nonprofit. We have no advertisers. Every dollar of revenue goes back into the verification infrastructure, auditor compensation, and creator grants. We exist to serve the people who make things — not to extract value from them.

Permanence as principle

Human creative work deserves a permanent record. Our public registry is immutable. A certified work remains certified — a timestamped marker in the historical record of what humans made, when, and who made it.
